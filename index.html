The Biography

A story still
being written.

Born in 2006 and raised in Birmingham, Alabama, Arlando Parker began learning the value of hands-on work at an early age. In 2013, at age seven, he willingly started working alongside his father, Ferlando Sr., owner of iHangTvs.

Over the years, that experience introduced him to practical skills, customer service, business operations, and the discipline behind blue-collar work. He credits those early lessons with helping shape his work ethic and his approach to new opportunities.

Arlando is also known for his ability to shift accents, voices, and speech patterns—often described as code-switching. That versatility became part of his creative identity and helped inspire his work in character-driven digital entertainment.

“The goal is bigger than a name. It’s about building something that lasts.”
